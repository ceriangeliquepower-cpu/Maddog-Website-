The Problem With How Most Wellness Programmes Work

Walk into most wellness bars or day spas and you'll find a laminated menu: a hydration drip here, a vitamin boost there, priced and packaged the same way for everyone who walks through the door. It feels good in the moment, but it rarely changes anything long-term because it was never designed around your biology in the first place.

GENESIS™ starts from a different premise entirely. It treats the body as one connected system, where mitochondrial health, hormones, metabolism, inflammation and brain chemistry are all talking to each other constantly. Fix one in isolation and the others often stay stuck. Address the underlying cellular environment, and everything built on top of it — energy, focus, body composition, resilience — has a chance to improve together.

Restore the cell, restore the person.

Who GENESIS™ Is Actually For

You don't need a diagnosis to benefit from this programme, and you don't need to be an elite athlete either. GENESIS™ was designed for men and women aged 30–70 who recognise themselves in symptoms like:

  • Persistent fatigue or burnout that sleep doesn't fix
  • Brain fog, poor concentration or that "flat" mental state
  • Slow or incomplete recovery after training or work stress
  • Stubborn weight gain, insulin resistance or early metabolic warning signs
  • Chronic low-grade inflammation
  • Hormonal shifts that come with ageing
  • A general sense of running at 70% instead of 100%

If any of that sounds familiar, you're exactly who this programme was built for — people who are still functioning, but who know they could be functioning far better, and who want to get ahead of disease rather than wait for it.

Inside the Four Pillars of GENESIS™

What makes GENESIS™ different from a standard IV menu is that it's structured — every element supports the next. It's built on four clinical pillars, each targeting a different layer of biological function.

1. Cellular Restoration

High-dose NAD+ therapy supports ATP production, mitochondrial repair, DNA repair pathways and recovery from fatigue — paired where appropriate with magnesium, vitamin C, B-complex, B12, amino acids and glutathione, delivered intravenously for full bioavailability.

2. Metabolic Intelligence

Focused on how efficiently your body produces, stores and uses energy — including MOTS-c for insulin sensitivity and fat oxidation, and where clinically appropriate, GLP-1 for body fat reduction and cardiometabolic markers. Every peptide protocol is SAPHRA-aligned and doctor-assessed.

3. Neurocognitive Optimisation

Targeted neuro-supportive peptides such as Semax (focus, memory, executive function) and Selank (emotional regulation, stress resilience, sleep quality) — often the pillar that delivers the most noticeable day-to-day difference.

4. Precision Longevity

IV nutrient therapy, peptide therapeutics, InBody body composition analysis, pathology interpretation, nutrition prescription and lifestyle optimisation, coordinated into one monitored, continuously adjusted plan.

Ballito has plenty of wellness options, but very few combine medical oversight, peptide therapeutics, IV nutrient therapy and body composition science under one clinically governed programme. GENESIS™ does, and it's built on a specific philosophy: prevention over reaction.
