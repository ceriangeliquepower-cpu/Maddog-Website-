Common Questions

Wellness FAQ.

Is Maddog Health & Wellness the same as the Maddog gym? +
They're connected but separate — Maddog Health & Wellness is a clinically supervised wellness facility, part of the Maddog Performance Institute in Ballito. You don't need a gym membership to use any wellness service.
Who administers the treatments? +
Every protocol is prescribed and overseen by our clinical team: Dr. Deo Du Plessis (Medical Doctor), Sister Amanda "Wren" Kobus (Registered Nurse Practitioner) and Robin JJ Williams (Sports Physiotherapist).
What services does Maddog Health & Wellness offer? +
IV drip therapy, body composition and slimming (InBody scanning, doctor-prescribed weight management, peptide therapy), recovery (ice bath and infrared sauna), sports physiotherapy, and personal training with a referral path to nutritional support.
Do I need to be a gym member to use the wellness clinic? +
No. Every wellness service is open to the public — no Maddog Performance Institute gym membership required.
Where is Maddog Health & Wellness located? +
22 Sandra Road, Balvista Centre, Ballito, KwaZulu-Natal. We're open Monday to Friday 06:00–20:00 and Saturday 07:00–14:00.
How do I book a consultation? +
Book online through Fresha, or head to our pricing page to start a consultation request for any service — IV therapy, body composition, recovery, physiotherapy or personal training.
