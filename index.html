Personal Training

Personal Training,
Built Around You.

Small, semi-private sessions of 2–5 people, full individual attention, at the same facility our fight team trains in. Personal Training at Maddog is coached by Tristan Botha and built entirely around your goals, whether that's strength, fat loss, conditioning, or just getting started. Prefer to train alone? 1-on-1 sessions are available on request.

No fighting or martial arts experience needed — the majority of our personal training clients are here purely for strength, fitness and fat loss, and never set foot in the cage.

  • Small, semi-private groups (2–5 people) with full individual attention
  • A programme built around your goals — strength, fat loss or conditioning
  • Full access to the same facility our fight team trains in
  • Free consultation before you commit to anything
