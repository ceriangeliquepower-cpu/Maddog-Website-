Infrared Sauna

The science of deep heat.

Infrared sauna therapy is one of the most evidence-backed recovery protocols available — athletes, executives and wellness-focused individuals worldwide have made it a non-negotiable part of their weekly routine. Unlike traditional steam saunas, infrared heat penetrates directly into muscle tissue rather than just heating the surrounding air.

Research consistently shows that regular infrared sauna use reduces delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS), improves sleep quality, reduces cortisol levels, and delivers measurable improvements in mood, focus and energy — often within the first session.

The Protocol

How the sauna works.

Settle in, breathe steadily and let the infrared heat work through your muscle tissue. There's no need to sit through unbearable heat — infrared saunas run at a lower, more comfortable ambient temperature than steam saunas while penetrating deeper into the body.

For first-time users, our team will guide you through the protocol. Most clients complete a session in 20–30 minutes and report feeling relaxed, clear and calm for hours afterward.

  • Recommended: 2–3 sessions per week for optimal results
  • First session includes protocol guidance
  • No experience necessary — all fitness levels welcome
  • Towels and changing facilities available

Common Questions

Infrared Sauna FAQ.

How hot is the infrared sauna? +
Our infrared sauna runs between 60°C and 80°C — lower than a traditional steam sauna, but the infrared heat penetrates deeper into muscle tissue for a more intense effect at a more comfortable ambient temperature.
How long should a session last? +
Most sessions run 20–30 minutes. First-timers can start shorter and build up as they acclimatise to the heat.
What's the difference between infrared and a regular sauna? +
Infrared saunas use light to heat the body directly rather than heating the surrounding air. This means deeper tissue penetration, a more intense detox and recovery response, and a lower, more comfortable ambient temperature than a traditional steam sauna.
How much does a single session cost? +
A standalone infrared sauna session is from R150. It can be combined with an ice bath as a full contrast therapy protocol for R220, or accessed unlimited via a monthly membership from R900.
Do I need to be a gym member? +
No — the infrared sauna is open to everyone, no gym membership required.
What are the benefits of infrared sauna sessions? +
Deep muscle relaxation and tension release, accelerated removal of metabolic waste, triggered heat shock proteins for cellular repair, supported cardiovascular health, improved skin condition, and reduced cortisol for better hormonal balance.
