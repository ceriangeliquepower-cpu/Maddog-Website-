Sports Physiotherapy

Assess. Treat. Perform.

Sports Physiotherapy focuses on the prevention, assessment, treatment and rehabilitation of sports and exercise related injuries. Our registered sports physiotherapist works with professional and recreational athletes to restore function, improve performance, reduce injury risk and support safe return to sport.

Services include injury diagnosis, manual therapy, rehabilitation programmes, strengthening & conditioning, movement screening, injury prevention and return-to-sport testing.
