Meet Amanda Kobus: The Coach Bringing Clinical Precision and Competitive Fire to Your Training

Maddog Performance Institute ◆ May 2026 ◆ 10 min read

Strength & Conditioning Coach  |  Powerlifting Athlete  |  Bodybuilding Athlete  |  Registered Nurse Practitioner  |  Ballito, KZN North Coast

At Maddog Performance Institute in Ballito, we believe that great coaching is built on three things: science, experience, and the courage to lead by example. Amanda Kobus embodies all three. As a Strength and Conditioning Coach, competitive powerlifting athlete, NPC Worldwide-titled bodybuilding athlete, and fully registered Nurse Practitioner, Amanda brings a combination of credentials to the coaching floor that is genuinely rare — not just in South Africa, but anywhere. She is the in-house clinician at Maddog Performance Institute, Ballito, and one of the most uniquely qualified coaches on the KZN North Coast.

If you are searching for a personal trainer in Ballito, a women's strength coach on the KZN North Coast, or an online strength and conditioning coach in South Africa who actually understands the human body at a clinical level — you have found her.

From Operating Theatres to the Lifting Platform: Amanda's Extraordinary Background

Amanda Kobus is not a conventional strength coach. Before she built her reputation in the gym, she built a career at the intersection of medicine, science, and service. As a fully registered Professional Nurse Practitioner (SANC Number: 14662043 | Practice Number: 1196763), a Medical Science Liaison, and a Military Medical Professional, Amanda accumulated decades of clinical experience that most coaches will never access.

This background is not a footnote to her coaching career — it is the foundation of it. Amanda understands human anatomy at a clinical level. She understands how the body responds to physical stress, how hormonal cycles affect strength output and recovery, how injury prevention integrates with programme design, and how an individual's medical history must shape every phase of their training. When Amanda writes your programme, she is not guessing. She is applying medical science to every rep, every set, and every progression.

Now in-house at Maddog Performance Institute, Ballito, Amanda merges her clinical background with elite-level coaching to deliver results that are safe, intelligent, and performance-driven.

A Competitive Athlete With Stage-Proven Results

Amanda does not just coach competitive athletes. She is one.

As a competitive powerlifting athlete, Amanda trains the squat, bench press, and deadlift with the technical rigour and structured periodisation that the sport demands. Her approach to strength is built on progressive overload, disciplined movement quality, and long-term performance planning — the same principles she delivers to every client she coaches.

On the bodybuilding stage, Amanda has performed at an elite level. She is a competitive NPC Worldwide athlete who won both the Bikini and Figure categories at the 2021 NPC Worldwide Jozi Regional Qualifier in South Africa — one of the most competitive regional NPC qualifiers on the South African calendar. Winning two categories at a single qualifier is not a small achievement. It requires peak physical preparation, months of structured nutrition and training, and the mental discipline to perform under pressure.

As a mother of three, Amanda achieved those results while navigating the real demands of family life. That is not a detail in her story — it is the point of her story.

Every client Amanda coaches benefits from both sides of her athlete identity: the clinical precision of her medical training, and the hard-won wisdom of someone who has stood on stage and delivered.

Coaching Anyone Who Is Serious About Getting Stronger

Amanda coaches men and women across all fitness levels and goals. Strength is not defined by gender — and neither is Amanda's approach. Whether you are a young athlete building your foundation, a working professional looking to regain fitness, or a seasoned competitor chasing a new personal best, she has the clinical knowledge and competitive experience to build a programme that works for you. Her background as a Nurse Practitioner means she reads every client through a medical lens: how the body responds to training stress, how recovery is shaped by sleep, nutrition, and lifestyle, how injury history must influence programme design, and how to consistently produce results without burning the client out.

Amanda's coaching empowers every client to:

  • Build real, functional strength — not just aesthetic results
  • Train safely and intelligently, with programmes grounded in medical and performance science
  • Train around life's demands — injuries, busy schedules, and changing goals — with a programme that adapts
  • Develop a performance mindset that carries beyond the gym into everyday life
  • Compete on stage or on the platform if that is the goal — or simply feel powerful and capable every day

Whether you are a busy professional on the KZN North Coast looking for structured strength training, someone returning to fitness after time away, or a competitive athlete preparing for an NPC show or powerlifting meet in South Africa — Amanda meets you exactly where you are.

In-Person, Online & Hybrid Coaching — Available Across South Africa

Amanda offers three flexible coaching formats, designed to fit how you actually live:

In-Person Coaching — Ballito

Train directly with Amanda at Maddog Performance Institute in Ballito. Access premium equipment, hands-on technique coaching, and real-time feedback in every session. This is the full coaching experience in one of the KZN North Coast's premier performance facilities.

Online Coaching — South Africa & Beyond

For clients across South Africa and internationally, Amanda's online strength coaching programmes deliver the same clinical rigour and personalised programming — digitally. Custom training plans, regular check-ins, nutritional guidance, and direct access to Amanda's medical and athletic expertise, without geographic limits.

Hybrid Coaching — The Best of Both

A combination of in-person sessions at Maddog Performance Institute, Ballito, and structured remote programming support. Ideal for clients who value the accountability of face-to-face training with the flexibility of remote coaching between sessions.

The Maddog Difference: Medical Insight Meets Elite Performance

Maddog Performance Institute was built on the belief that high performance and long-term health are not competing forces — they are the same goal, pursued intelligently. Having Amanda Kobus as an in-house registered clinician and coach is a direct expression of that philosophy.

Amanda's clients can expect:

  • Increased strength, lean muscle mass, and athletic performance
  • Improved movement quality, joint health, and injury resilience
  • Better body composition and long-term metabolic health
  • Structured competition preparation for NPC bodybuilding and powerlifting events
  • Mental discipline, performance mindset development, and sustainable lifestyle habits

Her clinical background means she can identify when something is not right, adapt programming around health conditions, and ensure every client is training in a way that supports their long-term wellbeing — not just chasing short-term results.

Ready to Train With Amanda Kobus at Maddog Performance Institute?

Whether you are chasing a powerlifting total, preparing for your first NPC competition in South Africa, returning to training after life got in the way, or simply ready to build a stronger, healthier version of yourself — Amanda Kobus has the credentials, the experience, and the drive to get you there.

She is not just a coach. She is a registered clinician, a competitive athlete, a mother, and someone who has done the work herself. That is who you want in your corner.
