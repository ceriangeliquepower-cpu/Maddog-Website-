Common Questions

NAD+ IV FAQ.

What is NAD+ and why does it decline with age? +
NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is a coenzyme in every living cell, essential for energy production, DNA repair and cellular communication. By your 40s, natural levels have declined by up to 50% — IV delivery restores them directly, bypassing the absorption limits of oral supplements.
How long does a NAD+ IV session take? +
NAD+ protocols take longer than standard IVs — typically 90 minutes to 3 hours for a full infusion, depending on dose. You can relax in our clinic space for the duration.
What's the difference between NAD+ IV, NAD+ Energy and NAD+ Anti-Aging? +
NAD+ IV (R1,300) is the standard restorative dose. NAD+ Energy (R2,016) is a higher-dose formulation with an energy-amplifying co-factor blend for peak performance. NAD+ Anti-Aging (R2,016) combines NAD+ with sirtuin-activating cofactors specifically targeted at cellular repair and healthy ageing.
How often should I get NAD+ IV therapy? +
Most clients start with a single session to assess response, then move to a quarterly programme for sustained anti-ageing and longevity benefits. Dr. Du Plessis will recommend a schedule based on your goals and bloodwork.
Is NAD+ IV therapy safe? +
Yes, when administered by a qualified nurse practitioner in a clinical setting. Every NAD+ protocol at Maddog is prescribed by Dr. Du Plessis and administered by Sister Wren Kobus, with a health screening before every session.
Can NAD+ be combined with Glutathione or Cognitive IV? +
Yes — a Glutathione Push (R650) can be added to any drip for extra detox and skin-brightening benefit. Ask about combining protocols during your clinical assessment.
