Why It Matters

Strength Training
After 40.

Muscle mass doesn't stay constant with age — research shows adults typically lose a meaningful percentage of muscle mass per decade after 30, and that loss accelerates from your 40s onward. The same applies to bone density: as we age, bones naturally become less dense, raising the risk of fracture and osteoporosis later in life.

The research is consistent on what works against both: resistance training. Structured strength work is one of the most effective ways to preserve muscle, protect bone density, and maintain the strength and mobility that make everyday life easier — for men and women alike. It's not about training like you're 20 again. It's about training smart for where your body is now.

  • Preserves and rebuilds muscle mass lost naturally with age
  • Supports bone density and helps protect against age-related bone loss
  • Improves everyday strength, balance and mobility
  • Programming built around your body, not a generic template

For Women

Hormone Health,
Perimenopause & Menopause.

Declining estrogen during perimenopause and menopause speeds up both muscle and bone loss — many women notice real changes in strength, energy and body composition during this transition. Research consistently points to resistance training as one of the most effective tools for working against these changes, alongside its wider benefits for energy, mood and sleep.

This isn't about a quick fix — it's about building a training habit that genuinely supports your body through this life stage. If you'd prefer to train alongside other women going through the same thing, Tristan also runs a dedicated semi-private women's class at 9am, built around exactly this approach.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Do I need gym experience to start personal training?
No. Every programme starts from an assessment of where you are right now, whether that's your first time in a gym or you're returning after time off. Tristan builds the plan around your actual starting point.
How is personal training different from a group class?
Sessions are small and semi-private — 2 to 5 people, each on their own programme, with programming, pacing and technique correction built around the individual rather than a fixed routine shared by a large class.
Can I book a private 1-on-1 session?
Yes. While standard sessions are semi-private (2–5 people), fully private 1-on-1 training is available on request — just get in touch and we'll work with you to find a time and set up sessions privately.
Is there a class specifically for women going through perimenopause or menopause?
Yes. Tristan runs a dedicated semi-private women's class at 9am, built around strength training for this life stage — supporting muscle mass, bone density and overall strength as hormone levels change. It runs on the same pricing as our standard semi-private sessions.
What does a personal training session include?
A structured session built around your goals — strength, fat loss, conditioning or general fitness — with hands-on coaching, technique correction, and a plan that progresses week to week.
How do you track my progress?
Every package includes a custom app programme, so your sessions, plan and progress are tracked in one place. Tristan reviews and adjusts your plan as you improve, so the programme keeps moving with you rather than staying static.
Can I combine personal training with other classes at Maddog?
Yes. Many members pair personal training sessions with MMA, BJJ, Kickboxing or Boxing classes as part of their broader training week.
How much does personal training cost?
Personal training starts from R1,600 per month for 2 sessions a week, up to R4,000 per month for 5 — each package includes a custom app program, plus a R200 drop-in rate for a single session. Your first consultation is free.
