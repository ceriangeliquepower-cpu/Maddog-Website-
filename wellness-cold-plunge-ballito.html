Ice Bath & Cold Plunge

The science of cold.

Cold water immersion is one of the most evidence-backed recovery protocols available — athletes, executives and wellness-focused individuals worldwide have made it a non-negotiable part of their weekly routine. The physiology is straightforward: cold constricts blood vessels, rapidly reducing inflammation and flushing metabolic waste.

Research consistently shows that regular cold immersion reduces delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS), improves sleep quality, reduces cortisol levels, and delivers measurable improvements in mood, focus and energy — often within the first session.

The Protocol

How the ice bath works.

A standard session is short and simple — submerge to at least chest height, focus on slow controlled breathing, and stay in for as long as is comfortable and safe. Our team guides every first-timer through the protocol before you get in.

Most clients start with 2–3 minutes and build tolerance over time. Sessions take 10–15 minutes including changing, and you'll feel the alertness and mood lift immediately afterward.

  • Recommended: 2–3 sessions per week for optimal results
  • First session includes protocol guidance
  • No experience necessary — all fitness levels welcome
  • Towels and changing facilities available

Common Questions

Ice Bath FAQ.

How cold is the ice bath at Maddog? +
Our cold plunge is maintained between 8°C and 12°C — the optimal range for physiological benefit without unnecessary risk.
How long should I stay in? +
We recommend starting with 2–3 minutes and building tolerance over time. Our team will guide first-timers through the protocol.
Do I need to be an athlete to use the ice bath? +
No — the ice bath is open to everyone, no gym membership or athletic background required. It's used by athletes and non-athletes alike for recovery, mood and stress benefits.
How much does a single session cost? +
A standalone ice bath session is from R90. It can also be combined with an infrared sauna session as a full contrast therapy protocol for R220, or accessed unlimited via a monthly membership from R900.
What are the real benefits of cold plunge therapy? +
Cold water immersion reduces acute and chronic inflammation, lowers DOMS after training, boosts norepinephrine by up to 300% (improving mood and focus), and improves sleep onset and quality.
Is cold plunge better alone or combined with sauna? +
Both work well on their own, but alternating between cold and heat creates a vascular pumping effect that produces greater results than cold alone — worth trying the combined protocol once you're comfortable with the ice bath by itself.
