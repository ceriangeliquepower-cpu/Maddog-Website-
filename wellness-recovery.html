Contrast Therapy

The science of hot and cold.

Contrast therapy — alternating between intense cold and dry heat — is one of the most evidence-backed recovery protocols available. Athletes, executives and wellness-focused individuals across the world have made it a non-negotiable part of their weekly routine. The physiology is straightforward: cold constricts blood vessels and reduces inflammation; heat dilates them and increases circulation. Alternating between the two creates a powerful pumping effect that accelerates recovery, reduces soreness, sharpens mental clarity and improves sleep quality.

Research consistently shows that regular contrast therapy reduces delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS) by up to 20%, improves sleep quality, reduces cortisol levels, and delivers measurable improvements in mood, focus and energy — often within the first session.

The Protocol

How contrast therapy works.

A standard contrast therapy session alternates between the cold plunge and the sauna in cycles — typically 3 rounds of cold followed by heat, ending cold. This cycling creates a vascular pumping effect unlike anything else available to the body without pharmacological support.

For first-time users, our team will guide you through the protocol. For regulars, you set your own pace. Most clients complete a session in 45–60 minutes and report feeling clear, calm and energised for the rest of the day.

Recovery FAQ.

How much does recovery cost? +
Standalone sessions start from R90 for the ice bath and R150 for infrared sauna. The combined contrast therapy protocol is R220. Monthly access runs from R900 (Basic, shared/unlimited) up to R3,000 (Elite, unlimited private VVIP).
Do I need to be a gym member to use recovery? +
No — the ice bath and infrared sauna are open to everyone, no Maddog Performance Institute gym membership or athletic background required.
What's the difference between the membership tiers? +
Basic Recovery (R900/month) gives unlimited sauna and ice sessions in shared space. Performance Recovery (R1,500/month) adds 8 private contrast sessions, priority booking and discounted add-ons. Elite Recovery (R3,000/month) is unlimited private VVIP use with discounted IV therapy and refreshments included.
Can I combine the ice bath and infrared sauna? +
Yes — alternating between cold and heat is our contrast therapy protocol, priced at R220 for a full 45-minute session. It's the most effective option once you're comfortable with the ice bath on its own.
Is recovery only for athletes? +
No. It's used by athletes and non-athletes alike for recovery, mood, stress and sleep benefits — no training background needed.
Do I need to book in advance? +
Booking is recommended, especially for the private membership tiers, to guarantee your slot. Book online through Fresha or via our pricing page.
What is contrast therapy? +
Contrast therapy is alternating between extreme cold (an ice bath) and intense heat (an infrared sauna). The rapid shift causes blood vessels to constrict and dilate in sequence, creating a pumping effect that reduces inflammation and speeds recovery beyond what either extreme does on its own.
Does cold plunge actually help recovery? +
Yes. Cold water immersion reduces acute and chronic inflammation, lowers DOMS (delayed onset muscle soreness) after training, and boosts norepinephrine by up to 300% — improving mood, focus and sleep quality alongside the physical recovery benefit.
