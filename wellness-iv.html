Common Questions

IV Therapy FAQ.

How much does IV therapy cost? +
Prices start at R600 for a Hydration IV, up to R1,400 for our Energy Performance IV. Most standard drips — Myers Cocktail, Recovery, Inflammation and Detox — sit at R1,300, with Jet Fuel and Immunity Boost at R1,100.
Who prescribes and administers the drips? +
Every drip is prescribed by Dr. Deo Du Plessis and administered by Sister Amanda "Wren" Kobus, a Registered Nurse Practitioner, in a clinical setting.
Is IV therapy safe? +
Yes. Every client receives a health screening before treatment, and drips are administered by a qualified nurse practitioner following a doctor's protocol.
Can I get a bespoke IV protocol? +
Yes. Following a review of your bloodwork and a clinical assessment with Dr. Du Plessis, a bespoke IV formulation can be designed specifically around your deficiencies, goals and health status.
Do I need to be a Maddog gym member? +
No — IV therapy is open to the public, no Maddog Performance Institute gym membership required.
Do you offer NAD+ IV therapy? +
Yes — NAD+ has its own dedicated longevity and anti-ageing protocol, separate from our standard drip menu. See our NAD+ & Longevity page for full pricing and details.
What is IV therapy? +
IV (intravenous) therapy delivers vitamins, minerals, fluids and other nutrients directly into your bloodstream through a small vein catheter, bypassing the digestive system entirely.
Why is IV therapy more effective than oral supplements? +
Oral supplements have to survive digestion before your body can absorb them, often losing much of their potency along the way. IV therapy delivers nutrients straight into the bloodstream for 100% bioavailability and immediate effect.
