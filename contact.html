Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do I need any experience to start? +

Not at all. At Maddog Performance Institute in Ballito, we work with everyone — from complete beginners to professional athletes. Whether it’s your first time stepping into a gym or you’re looking to level up your performance, our coaches tailor every session to your current fitness level. You’ll be guided every step of the way, so you feel confident, safe, and progressing from day one.

What should I bring to my first class? +

Keep it simple. Bring comfortable training clothes, a water bottle, and a positive mindset. If you’re joining MMA, BJJ, or kickboxing classes, we can guide you on gear as you progress. For your first session at our Ballito facility, just arrive ready to move — we’ll take care of the rest.

How much does membership cost? +

Membership at Maddog Performance Institute is designed to be flexible based on your goals. We offer options for group training, semi-private personal training, and performance packages that include access to our recovery suite and IV drip therapy. The best way to get accurate pricing is to chat with us directly — we’ll recommend the right plan based on your fitness level, schedule, and the results you want to achieve.

Do you have classes for kids? +

Yes, we do. Our kids’ classes focus on discipline, confidence, fitness, and self-defence in a structured and supportive environment. Whether it’s Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu or beginner martial arts, we create a space where kids can grow physically and mentally while having fun.

Is the gym open to women? +

Absolutely. Maddog Performance Institute is a welcoming, supportive environment for women of all fitness levels. We offer everything from beginner-friendly classes to semi-private personal training, as well as self-defence and strength programmes designed to build confidence, strength, and overall wellness.

What disciplines do you offer at Maddog Performance Institute? +

We offer a full range of performance-driven training in Ballito, including:

  • ◆MMA (Mixed Martial Arts)
  • ◆Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ)
  • ◆Kickboxing
  • ◆Powerlifting & Strength Training
  • ◆Personal Training
  • ◆Functional Fitness

On top of that, we have a dedicated recovery and performance suite, including sauna, ice bath, and IV drip therapy to help you recover faster and perform better.

Where exactly are you located in Ballito? +

Maddog Performance Institute is based in Ballito, KwaZulu-Natal, conveniently located for residents and surrounding areas on the North Coast. Once you book a session or reach out, we’ll send you the exact location details and directions to the facility.

Can I book recovery sessions without being a gym member? +

Yes — you don’t need to be a member to use our recovery services. Our recovery suite in Ballito is open to anyone looking to improve recovery, boost performance, or support overall wellness. This includes sauna sessions, ice baths, and professional IV drip therapy. It’s a standalone service, perfect for athletes, busy professionals, or anyone wanting to feel and perform better.
