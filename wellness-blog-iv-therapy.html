IV Drip Therapy: Benefits, Protocols & What to Expect

Maddog Health & Wellness ◆ April 2026 ◆ 8 min read

The Fastest Path to Optimal Nutrition Is Not Through Your Stomach

For decades, intravenous nutrient therapy was available only in hospitals and private medical clinics. Elite athletes and high-performance individuals have used it strategically for years — to accelerate recovery between training sessions, manage fatigue during demanding periods, and sustain performance at their best.

At Maddog Performance Health & Wellness in Ballito, IV drip therapy is a core pillar of our clinical wellness offering — supervised by Sister Wren Kobus, our registered nurse practitioner, and available to athletes, professionals, and anyone on the KZN North Coast who wants to optimise how their body responds to the demands placed on it.

What Is IV Drip Therapy and Why Is It More Effective Than Oral Supplements?

Intravenous (IV) drip therapy delivers vitamins, minerals, amino acids, electrolytes, and other essential nutrients directly into the bloodstream through an intravenous line. Unlike oral supplementation — which must pass through the digestive system and is subject to significant absorption limitations — IV delivery bypasses the gut entirely, achieving near-total bioavailability almost immediately.

This distinction matters more than most people appreciate. Even in a healthy digestive system, only a fraction of orally consumed nutrients is absorbed before excretion. The same dose delivered intravenously achieves plasma concentrations that oral supplementation simply cannot reach — a difference that is felt, not just measured.

The ceiling of your physical performance and daily energy is not determined by how much you take — it is set by how much your body can actually absorb and use.

The Core Benefits of IV Drip Therapy in Ballito

Accelerated Recovery After Intense Training or Physical Demand

High-intensity training creates significant demand on muscle tissue. IV drips formulated with magnesium, the B vitamin complex, glutathione, and targeted amino acids directly support the cellular repair mechanisms that follow intense exercise — often reducing recovery time in ways that passive rest alone cannot match.

Rapid, Complete Rehydration

Effective hydration is not simply a matter of drinking enough water. Cellular hydration requires the right balance of electrolytes to facilitate water entry into cells where it is physiologically needed. IV rehydration corrects electrolyte deficits rapidly and completely, restoring intracellular fluid balance in a way that drinking cannot achieve in the same timeframe.

Immune System Support During High-Demand Periods

Elevated training loads and chronic stress both suppress immune function. High-dose vitamin C delivered intravenously has substantial evidence for supporting immune competence — and at levels only achievable through IV delivery.

Improved Energy and Cognitive Clarity

B vitamin deficiencies — even subclinical depletions — are extremely common in active individuals and are directly associated with fatigue, cognitive fog, and reduced motivational drive. IV formulations containing the full B vitamin spectrum reliably produce noticeable improvements in energy and mental sharpness within hours of administration.

Detoxification and Antioxidant Restoration via Glutathione

Glutathione is the body's primary antioxidant compound — produced naturally but consistently depleted by intense training, chronic stress, and the ageing process. IV glutathione supplementation restores levels rapidly and has been associated with improved skin health, enhanced liver function, reduced systemic inflammation, and significantly faster recovery from oxidative stress.

What to Expect During Your IV Drip Session

All IV drip sessions at Maddog are administered by Sister Wren Kobus, our registered nurse practitioner. Before your first session, a brief health screening is conducted to confirm suitability and customise your formulation to your goals and health status.

  • Sessions typically take between 30 and 60 minutes (NAD+ protocols take longer)
  • You relax comfortably in our clinical wellness suite during infusion
  • Most clients notice measurable effects within hours — improved energy, reduced soreness, mental clarity
  • Booking in advance is strongly recommended as sessions are practitioner-supervised

How Often Should You Have IV Drip Therapy?

Frequency depends on your goals and lifestyle demands. Athletes in heavy training phases typically benefit from once-weekly sessions. For general wellness maintenance and energy optimisation, once or twice monthly is a common and effective approach. Your nurse practitioner will guide you based on your individual assessment.
