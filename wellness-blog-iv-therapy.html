IV Drip Therapy: Benefits, Protocols & What to Expect

Maddog Health & Wellness ◆ April 2026 ◆ 8 min read

The Fastest Path to Optimal Nutrition Is Not Through Your Stomach

Between work stress, poor sleep, travel, and the general grind of daily life, most people are running some kind of nutrient deficit long before it shows up as an obvious symptom — persistent fatigue, brain fog, an immune system that always seems one step behind. Oral supplements are a reasonable start, but a stressed, under-slept gut doesn't absorb what you swallow nearly as well as the label promises.

At Maddog Performance Health & Wellness in Ballito, IV drip therapy is a core pillar of our clinical wellness offering — supervised by Sister Wren Kobus, our registered nurse practitioner, and available to professionals, parents, athletes, and anyone on the KZN North Coast who wants their body running on more than caffeine and willpower.

Why IV Delivery Beats a Handful of Vitamins Every Morning

IV therapy delivers vitamins, minerals, amino acids and electrolytes straight into the bloodstream, bypassing digestion entirely. Even with a healthy gut, only a fraction of what you swallow actually gets absorbed before the rest is excreted — and that fraction drops further under stress, poor sleep, or illness, which is usually exactly when you need the nutrients most.

How you feel isn't determined by how much you take — it's determined by how much your body can actually absorb and use.

What IV Therapy Actually Changes for Wellness Clients

Steadier Energy Without the Crash

Not the jittery, short-lived lift of another coffee — a B-complex and magnesium formulation supports the cellular energy pathways that caffeine just borrows against. Clients typically describe it as feeling properly rested rather than artificially wired.

Rehydration for More Than Just Athletes

Effective hydration isn't just about drinking enough water — it's about having the right electrolyte balance to get that water into your cells. Travel, a hot Ballito summer, a rough night, or simply not drinking enough all week are all legitimate reasons to correct a deficit properly rather than sipping water and hoping.

Fewer Sick Days, Stronger Immune Resilience

Chronic stress suppresses immune function just as effectively as physical overtraining does — which is why the same office cold seems to knock some people out for a week and others for a day. High-dose vitamin C, delivered intravenously, has solid evidence behind it for supporting immune resilience through winter, travel season, or a run of bad sleep.

Mental Clarity for a Demanding Week

Subclinical B-vitamin depletion is extremely common and shows up as fatigue, decision fatigue, and a foggy head — not dramatic symptoms, just a persistent sense of running below capacity. A full B-complex IV reliably sharpens energy and focus within hours, which matters before a big presentation as much as before a big lift.

Glutathione for Skin, Ageing and Everyday Detox

Glutathione is the body's primary antioxidant — produced naturally, but steadily depleted by chronic stress and the ageing process rather than just by exercise. IV glutathione restores it quickly, and is associated with improved skin clarity, better liver function, and a genuine sense of feeling less run-down day to day.

What to Expect During Your IV Drip Session

All IV drip sessions at Maddog are administered by Sister Wren Kobus, our registered nurse practitioner. Before your first session, a brief health screening is conducted to confirm suitability and customise your formulation to your goals and health status.

  • Sessions typically take between 30 and 60 minutes (NAD+ protocols take longer)
  • You relax comfortably in our clinical wellness suite during infusion
  • Most clients notice measurable effects within hours — improved energy, reduced soreness, mental clarity
  • Booking in advance is strongly recommended as sessions are practitioner-supervised

How Often Should You Have IV Drip Therapy?

Frequency depends on your goals and lifestyle demands. Athletes in heavy training phases typically benefit from once-weekly sessions. For general wellness maintenance and energy optimisation, once or twice monthly is a common and effective approach. Your nurse practitioner will guide you based on your individual assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be an athlete or gym member to book an IV drip?

Not at all. The majority of our IV clients aren't training for anything — they're professionals, parents, and anyone wanting to feel and function better day to day. No referral, membership, or fitness level required.

Is IV drip therapy safe?

Administered by a registered nurse practitioner using pharmaceutical-grade nutrients in a clinical setting, IV therapy is very safe for most healthy adults. Every client is screened before their first session to confirm suitability.

Does it hurt?

Most clients describe it as no more uncomfortable than a standard blood draw — a brief pinch as the line is inserted, then nothing for the rest of the session.

Can I combine IV therapy with other wellness services?

Yes — many clients pair IV therapy with contrast therapy (cold plunge and infrared sauna) or a physiotherapy session as part of a broader recovery day.
