Cold Plunge & Sauna: The Science Behind Contrast Therapy

Maddog Health & Wellness ◆ April 2026 ◆ 8 min read

The Recovery Gap That Is Costing You Wellbeing

Most people are careful about diet, and a lot of people squeeze in some form of exercise. Almost nobody is deliberate about recovery — the kind that actually regulates stress hormones, inflammation, and sleep rather than just resting on the couch. That gap is where a surprising amount of everyday fatigue, poor sleep, and low-grade anxiety quietly builds up without an obvious cause.

At Maddog Performance Health & Wellness, cold plunge and infrared sauna are offered as a standalone clinical wellness service — no gym membership, training background, or fitness level required. Used on their own, each delivers a measurable physiological reset. Used together as a guided contrast protocol, the shift in how you feel afterwards is considerably more pronounced.

What Is Cold Plunge Therapy and Why Is It Used?

Cold plunge therapy — cold water immersion, typically 8 to 12 degrees Celsius for a short, controlled duration — has moved well beyond its old reputation as something only athletes do. The research behind it now covers stress physiology and nervous system regulation just as much as physical recovery, which is exactly why it sits inside a wellness clinic rather than only a gym.

The moment you go under, the body's response is immediate: blood vessels constrict, heart rate shifts, and the nervous system is pushed into a state of heightened alertness. What follows that initial shock is a cascade of effects that outlasts the few minutes you actually spend in the water.

What Cold Plunge Does for Everyday Wellbeing

  • Calms an overactive nervous system and lowers your baseline stress reactivity over time
  • A genuine, noticeable mood lift, driven by a natural release of norepinephrine
  • Support for healthy circulation and longer-term cardiovascular resilience
  • A sharpening of focus that carries with you well after you've dried off
  • Better resilience to the everyday physical and psychological stress that builds up between weekends
  • A structured, controlled way to build tolerance for discomfort — a skill that transfers surprisingly well to the rest of life

Infrared Sauna: Deep Heat, Real Results

Traditional saunas heat the air around you. Infrared saunas use infrared light to heat tissue directly at a cellular level, without pushing the room to an uncomfortable temperature — which is why a session can run longer and feel considerably more relaxing than a traditional sauna.

What Infrared Sauna Does for the Body

  • Deep relaxation for the chronically tight shoulders, neck, and lower back that come with desk work
  • Cardiovascular conditioning through regular, passive heat exposure
  • A genuine, sustained sweat-based detox
  • Measurably lower cortisol and a real reduction in day-to-day anxiety
  • Better sleep quality, particularly with an evening session
  • Visibly healthier skin, driven by increased circulation and cellular turnover

Why Cold Plunge and Sauna Work Better Together

Used separately, both are highly effective. Used in alternating contrast — moving between heat and cold in a structured, practitioner-guided protocol — the physiological effect compounds well beyond what either achieves alone. Contrast therapy is a recognised recovery and stress-management protocol worldwide, and it's a core offering at Maddog rather than an add-on.

The mechanism is straightforward: heat vasodilates blood vessels and raises core temperature, cold rapidly constricts those same vessels. That alternating expansion and contraction acts like a pump — supporting circulation and nutrient delivery throughout the body.

Your Contrast Therapy Options

Sessions range from a self-guided contrast soak through to a fully structured, coached protocol — and can be paired with an IV drip for a complete recovery session. Your practitioner will help you choose the right format based on what you're looking to get out of it, whether that's stress relief, better sleep, or simply an hour to yourself.

Even a single session produces a noticeable shift in how the body and mind feel afterwards. Most clients describe it as calm alertness — a state that's difficult to reach through any other means.

Who Should Be Using Contrast Therapy?

Professionals Managing Chronic Stress

Disrupted sleep, tension held in the neck and shoulders, and general burnout are legitimate reasons to invest in intentional recovery, with or without any training goal attached. Infrared sauna in particular has strong evidence for reducing systemic inflammation — a root driver of the fatigue and low mood that builds up over months of unmanaged stress.

Anyone Prioritising Sleep and Long-Term Health

Cold exposure and heat therapy both influence the neuroendocrine system — affecting cortisol and norepinephrine in ways that consistently support better sleep, steadier mood, and greater resilience to daily stress. Regular contrast therapy is one of the more effective non-pharmaceutical interventions available for this.

Anyone New to Recovery Work

You don't need a training background or a specific fitness goal to start. Most first-time clients come in simply curious about the wellness trend and stay because of how they feel afterwards — this is designed to be approachable for a first-timer, not intimidating.

Frequently Asked Questions

What water temperature do you use for cold plunge?

Our plunge sits in the 8–12°C range — the zone with the best evidence behind it for nervous-system and stress benefits. Going colder isn't more effective; consistent, regular exposure matters far more than extreme temperature.

Do I need to be fit, or a gym member, to try this?

No. Contrast therapy at Maddog Health & Wellness is open to everyone, regardless of fitness level or gym membership. Many of our clients have never set foot in a gym — they're here purely for the stress, sleep, and wellbeing benefits.

How often should I book a session?

Three to four sessions a week is enough for most clients to notice a measurable difference. If you're going through a particularly demanding or stressful period, more frequent sessions can be worthwhile.

Is this safe if I have a medical condition?

Cold plunge and infrared sauna are well tolerated by most healthy adults, but if you have a cardiovascular condition, are pregnant, have Raynaud's, or are otherwise medically sensitive to heat or cold, speak to your doctor first and let our team know before your session — we'll adjust the protocol accordingly.
