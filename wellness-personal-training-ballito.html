The Philosophy Training Through Every Stage

Strength isn't
automatic.

Muscle mass doesn't stay constant with age — research shows adults typically lose a meaningful percentage of muscle mass per decade after 30, and that loss accelerates from your 40s onward. The same applies to bone density. Structured strength training is one of the most effective ways to work against both.

This isn't about a quick fix — it's about building a training habit that genuinely supports your body through whatever stage of life you're in, whoever you are.

If you'd prefer to train alongside other women going through the same thing, Tristan also runs a dedicated semi-private women's class at 9am, built around this same approach. It's one option among several — personal training itself is open to everyone.

Common Questions

Personal Training FAQ.

Is this only for MMA and combat sports? +
No. Personal training with Tristan is separate from the combat sports programme — it's general strength, conditioning and habit-building work for everyday goals, open to complete beginners as well as experienced gym-goers.
How does the training app work? +
Every personal training package includes a custom programme built and tracked through an app, so your sessions, plan and progress all live in one place. Tristan reviews your results and adjusts your programme as you improve, whether you train in person or follow sessions remotely.
Do personal trainers give nutritional advice? +
Tristan's strength and conditioning coaching is training-only — if you'd like nutritional support alongside it, we'll refer you to our Slimming Clinic. Amanda Wren Kobus is different: as a Registered Nurse Practitioner, nutritionally-informed guidance is built directly into her coaching, alongside her powerlifting and strength programming.
Should I train with Tristan or Amanda? +
Both are certified, results-driven coaches with different strengths. Tristan specialises in general strength, conditioning and sustainable habit-building, with app-based programming for every fitness level. Amanda Wren Kobus is a Registered Nurse Practitioner and SA Powerlifting Federation certified coach, ideal if you want powerlifting-specific coaching or nutritionally-informed guidance built into your sessions. Book a consultation and we'll help you decide.
Can I get bloodwork or an InBody scan through personal training? +
Not directly through personal training. Bloodwork, InBody body composition scanning and nutritional support are all handled through our Slimming Clinic under Dr. Deo Du Plessis — we'll refer you there for an assessment, at an additional fee separate from your PT package.
How much does personal training cost? +
From R1,600 per month for 2 sessions a week, up to R4,000 per month for 5 sessions a week, each including a custom app programme. Single drop-in sessions are R200.
Is there a women-only training option? +
Personal training is open to everyone and built around individual goals, not gender. For clients who'd prefer to train alongside other women, Tristan runs a semi-private women's class at 9am, and Amanda Wren Kobus — a mother of three and competitive bodybuilding athlete — offers a coaching style many women choose for her lived understanding of the female body and its demands.
Do I need to be a Maddog gym member? +
No — personal training is a standalone service. You don't need an existing Maddog gym membership to book sessions with Tristan.
I'd rather train inside a performance gym environment — is that available? +
Yes. If you'd prefer personal training inside our MMA and strength-sports facility instead of this standalone wellness studio, Tristan Botha also coaches personal training at Maddog Performance Institute — same coach, different setting.
