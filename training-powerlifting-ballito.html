Powerlifting & Strength Training
In Ballito.

Powerlifting at Maddog Performance Institute is coached with the same precision and discipline that builds world champions. Our powerlifting programme is built around the three competition lifts — squat, bench press, and deadlift — and is structured to develop maximal strength, technical mastery, and competition-ready performance.

Whether you are stepping onto a platform for the first time or chasing a total PB, our coaches provide the technical expertise, periodised programming, and performance environment to take your lifting to the next level.
  • Squat, bench press and deadlift — technical coaching from the ground up
  • Periodised strength programming — peaking for competition or personal bests
  • Competition preparation and meet-day coaching
  • Accessory work for strength development and injury prevention
  • Nutrition guidance to support strength and body composition goals

Why Train Powerlifting

The Benefits of
Strength Training.

Powerlifting isn't just about the platform — it's one of the most effective long-term investments you can make in your body.

Strength
Real, Measurable Progress
Your squat, bench and deadlift numbers don't lie — few training methods give you such a clear, honest measure of progress.
Longevity
Bone & Joint Health
Loaded strength training builds bone density and joint resilience — a genuine investment in how your body ages.
Body Composition
Metabolic Health
Building and maintaining muscle mass through strength training improves metabolic health and body composition long-term.
Safety
Injury Prevention
Proper technique under load — coached, not guessed at — builds resilience that protects you in everyday life and other sports.
Confidence
Earned Physical Capability
There's a particular kind of confidence that comes from knowing exactly what your body is capable of lifting.
Pathway
Competition, If You Want It
Full competition preparation and meet-day coaching are available under a certified SA Powerlifting Federation coach.

Nurse Practitioner & Bodybuilding Athlete

Coached by a Protea-Selected
Athlete & KZN's Strongest M1.

Amanda Wren Kobus is a Proteas-selected Master 1 athlete and KZN's strongest Master 1 competitor — currently ranked South Africa's third-strongest master athlete. She's also Director of the KwaZulu-Natal Powerlifting Association and a certified Coach and Referee under the South African Powerlifting Federation, bringing institutional knowledge and elite competitive experience to every strength session she leads. Her clinical background as a Registered Nurse Practitioner informs every training prescription — she understands the body not just as an athlete, but as a medical professional.

On the bodybuilding stage, Amanda competes at an elite level as an NPC Worldwide athlete — she won both the Bikini and Figure categories at the 2021 NPC Worldwide Jozi Regional Qualifier, one of the most competitive regional qualifiers on the South African calendar. A mother of three, she brings a deep understanding of the female body, real-world demands, and the mindset required to build strength around life's responsibilities.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Is powerlifting only for people who want to compete?
No. Most clients train powerlifting purely for strength, body composition and long-term health — competition preparation is available for those who want it, but it's not required.
Do I need experience with the squat, bench and deadlift to start?
No. Coaching starts from the ground up with technical fundamentals, regardless of your current experience level, and builds from there.
Is powerlifting safe?
Yes, when coached properly. Sessions are led by Amanda Wren Kobus, a Registered Nurse Practitioner and SA Powerlifting Federation certified Coach and Referee, so programming and technique are built around your body and medical background.
Can I train powerlifting with a partner or in a group?
Yes. Alongside 1-on-1 personal training, couples training and small group training (groups of 5, 2x or 3x per week) are both available.
How much does powerlifting coaching cost?
Personal training starts from R300 per drop-in session, with monthly packages of 8 or 12 sessions. Couples training is R600 per session, and small group training runs R2,200–R3,300 per person per month.
