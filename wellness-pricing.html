Pricing Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does IV drip therapy cost in Ballito?

Our IV protocols start from R600 for a Hydration IV, with Jet Fuel and Recovery IVs at R1,100–R1,300, and bespoke or add-on formulations from R600. Full pricing is listed above.

How much does contrast therapy (cold plunge and sauna) cost?

Standalone sessions start from R90 for the cold plunge and R150 for the infrared sauna. Our Contrast Combo — both together in one ~45-minute protocol — is R220.

How much does an InBody scan cost?

A drop-in scan with printed results is R165. A full scan with results interpretation and a goal-alignment consultation is R450.

How much does the slimming clinic cost?

A full doctor assessment is R950. Follow-up protocol consultations, once you're on a plan, are R500.

How much does sports physiotherapy cost?

An initial consultation is R750 (R650 for Maddog Performance Institute athletes), with 45-minute follow-up appointments at R700 (R650 for Maddog athletes).

Do I need a membership to use these services?

No — every service on this page is available pay-as-you-go with no membership required. An Unlimited Recovery Membership from R900/month is also available if you plan to use the recovery suite regularly.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, EFT and card payments (Visa and Mastercard via Yoco) are all accepted in person at the clinic.
