What Is Sports Physiotherapy

Not just physio.
Sport-specific physio.

Sports physiotherapy is a specialised branch of physiotherapy built around what physical performance actually demands of the body — how muscles, joints, tendons and the nervous system respond under load, stress and repeated movement. It goes beyond treating an injury after it happens: the goal is diagnosing it accurately, treating it properly, and building the resilience to stop it recurring.

At Maddog, that means working with a physiotherapist who has spent over a decade doing exactly this at the highest level of professional sport — not a generalist clinic offering physio as one of many services.

Sports physiotherapy focuses on the prevention, assessment, treatment and rehabilitation of sports and exercise-related injuries. Whether you're a professional athlete or someone who moved wrong unloading the car — pain and restricted movement affect quality of life. Robin JJ Williams is here to fix it.

Services include injury diagnosis, manual therapy, rehabilitation programmes, strengthening and conditioning, movement screening, injury prevention and return-to-sport testing. Maddog athletes receive preferential rates on all consultations.

Common Questions

Physiotherapy FAQ.

Do I need a referral? +
No referral is needed. You can book directly with Robin JJ Williams — online, via WhatsApp, or through our contact page.
What conditions does Robin treat? +
Sports and exercise-related injuries of all kinds — muscle tears, ligament sprains, joint instability, overuse injuries, chronic pain and post-surgical rehabilitation. He also has specialist training in concussion management for young athletes.
How much does a consultation cost? +
An initial consultation is R750 (R650 for Maddog Performance Institute athletes), with 45-minute follow-up appointments at R700 (R650 for Maddog athletes).
Does Robin treat children and teenage athletes? +
Yes. Robin works with all ages and fitness levels, and has particular expertise in youth rugby injuries and concussion management through his ongoing Master's research at UCT.
Can Robin come to me instead? +
Yes — home visits are available in Ballito and surrounding areas within 5km, ideal for post-surgical patients, elderly clients or those with limited mobility.
When should I see a physio instead of waiting it out? +
If pain persists beyond 48–72 hours after exercise, changes the way you move, keeps recurring in the same area, or follows an acute injury with swelling or loss of range of motion — it's worth getting assessed rather than waiting.
