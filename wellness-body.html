Common Questions

Body & Weight FAQ.

How much does the slimming clinic cost? +
The initial Doctor Assessment is R950, and follow-up Slimming Protocol Consultations are R500. Every programme starts with an InBody scan, from R165 — see our InBody Scan page for full scan pricing.
Is the slimming programme medically supervised? +
Yes. It's not a diet programme — it's a medically supervised body transformation protocol. Every client begins with a doctor assessment, and your programme is prescribed by Dr. Deo Du Plessis and monitored by our clinical team.
What's included in a slimming protocol? +
A full medical assessment, an individualised weight management plan, clinically monitored progress tracking, nutritional support and eating plan integration, and optional IV therapy to support fat metabolism.
Is peptide therapy available? +
Yes — SAPHRA-approved peptide protocols are available, prescribed by Dr. Du Plessis. Pricing is by consultation, since every protocol is designed around your individual assessment.
Do I need an InBody scan before starting? +
Yes — every body composition programme starts with an InBody scan, a two-minute hospital-grade breakdown of your muscle mass, fat mass, water and bone.
Do I need to be a Maddog gym member? +
No — the slimming clinic, InBody scanning and peptide therapy are all open to the public, no Maddog Performance Institute gym membership required.
What is body composition, and why does it matter more than weight alone? +
Body composition breaks your weight down into muscle mass, fat mass, water and bone, rather than a single number on a scale. Two people can weigh exactly the same and have very different health profiles depending on that breakdown.
How does an InBody scan measure body composition? +
InBody scanning uses bioelectrical impedance analysis — a safe, painless electrical signal — to map your body composition with medical-grade accuracy in under two minutes, the same technology used in hospitals and elite sports science facilities.
