The Benefits of
Kickboxing.

Kickboxing delivers one of the most efficient full-body workouts available, while building real striking skill at the same time. Here's what regular training builds.

Fitness
Cardio & Fat Loss
High-intensity rounds of striking, pad work and footwork burn serious calories while building genuine cardiovascular capacity.
Coordination
Speed & Reflexes
Combination work and defensive drilling sharpen hand-eye coordination, reaction time and footwork under pressure.
Stress Relief
A Powerful Outlet
Few workouts clear your head like hitting pads with full intent — kickboxing is one of the best stress-release sessions on the schedule.
Self-Defence
Real Striking Skill
Technique tested in real competitive MMA, not choreographed — a genuinely useful stand-up skillset.
Strength
Full-Body Power
Kicks and strikes generate power from the ground up, building functional strength through the hips, core and legs.
Community

Group classes with a coach who pushes you and training partners who keep you accountable, session after session.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Do I need to be fit before I start kickboxing?
No — this is one of the biggest myths about kickboxing. Every drill can be scaled to your current fitness level, and the training itself is what builds your fitness. You don't need to arrive in shape; you'll get there by training.
Is kickboxing full-contact?
Pad work, bag work and partner drilling are the core of every class. Sparring is introduced progressively, always structured and supervised, and is optional — many members train purely for fitness and technique.
Do I need my own gloves or equipment?
No — gloves and pads are available on-site for your first trial class. Once you're training regularly we can help you get properly fitted kit.
Is kickboxing available for kids?
Yes. Coach Lucky works with both kids and adults across all experience levels, so younger members can train kickboxing fitness alongside our adult classes.
What's the difference between Kickboxing and Boxing Fitness at Maddog?
Kickboxing includes kicks, clinch work and a broader striking toolkit built on real MMA competition experience. Boxing Fitness is hands-only — pad and bag work with no kicking or contact — for a fast, focused cardio workout.
How much does kickboxing cost at Maddog?
Kickboxing membership is R800 per month for a single discipline, R1,400 per month for unlimited access to all martial arts classes, or R150 per drop-in class with no commitment. Your first class is free.
