Private Coaching

Private Boxing Coaching.
One-on-One Attention.

The personal attention and encouragement of a private session can help you stay motivated and consistent — particularly when working towards specific goals like weight management, improved fitness, competition preparation or technical development.

One-on-one training lets your coach closely monitor technique, fatigue and intensity throughout the session, with every exercise modified to your experience, physical ability and goals. That makes private coaching suitable for complete beginners, fitness clients and competitive athletes alike — provided the training is appropriately structured and supervised.

  • Personalised pace and programming, built entirely around you
  • Real, focused pad work — not shared time in a group class
  • Available as 1-on-1, semi-private (3 people), or private padwork
  • Suitable from complete beginner through to competitive athlete

Private sessions run on the same rates as our Olympic Boxing Programme — 1-on-1 Private Training from R350/hour, Semi Private from R600/hour, and Private Padwork from R700/hour. View full private training pricing.

Why Train Boxing

The Benefits of
Boxing.

Boxing is one of the most complete conditioning tools there is — here's what it builds, whether you're training for fitness or competition.

Fitness
Cardio & Fat Loss
Rounds of pad work and bag work deliver one of the most efficient calorie-burning workouts available, no equipment learning curve required.
Coordination
Hand-Eye Speed
Combination drilling sharpens reflexes, timing and hand speed in a way few other workouts replicate.
Confidence
Real, Earned Skill
Learning genuine boxing technique — not just throwing punches — builds a quiet, practical kind of self-belief.
Stress Relief
A Proper Reset
Hitting pads with full intent is one of the most effective ways to switch off from work and daily stress for an hour.
Strength
Full-Body Conditioning
Boxing engages your legs, core and shoulders every round — functional strength built through real movement, not machines.
Pathway
Room to Go Further
If fitness turns into ambition, Coach Marcele's competitive programme is right there — no need to switch gyms or coaches.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Do I need experience to join Boxing Fitness?
No. Boxing Fitness is beginner-friendly — coaches take you through every technique from scratch, and you'll train alongside members at every level. No prior boxing experience is required.
Is Boxing Fitness full-contact?
No. Boxing Fitness classes are pad work, bag work and combination drills — no sparring, no contact. It's real boxing technique used purely for conditioning.
Is there a competitive boxing pathway at Maddog?
Yes. Coach Marcele has more than two decades of involvement in boxing, developing fighters from foundations through to elite amateur and professional competition, including national-level champions. Athletes who want to compete are supported through full fight preparation.
Do you offer private or 1-on-1 boxing coaching?
Yes. Private, semi-private and private padwork sessions are available on the same rates as our Olympic Boxing Programme — R350 per hour for 1-on-1, R600 per hour for semi-private (3 people), and R700 per hour for private padwork. Sessions are suitable for complete beginners through to competitive athletes.
Do you offer boxing for women or kids?
This page covers our general and competitive boxing programme. We also run dedicated women's/moms boxing and kids boxing classes — ask our team for current times and enrolment.
What equipment do I need for my first class?
Nothing — gloves and hand wraps are available on-site for your first trial class. No equipment purchase required to get started.
How much does Boxing Fitness cost?
Boxing Fitness membership is R890 per month (2x per week), or R150 per drop-in class with no commitment. Our competitive Olympic Boxing Programme has its own pricing, starting from R890 per month for group classes.
