## Combat sports / martial arts
- **Ringside Boxing Gym** (ringsideboxing.co.za) — weak on-page SEO, generic page slugs, no location keyword in URLs. Wins on off-page signals (reviews, brand recognition), not content. Beats Maddog on pure boxing terms.
- **CombatCoaching.com** (combatcoaching.com) — sophisticated multi-location franchise (Ballito, Amanzimtoti, Mauritius), 37 pages, one page per discipline (`/kids-bjj/`, `/kids-mma/`, `/bully-proof/`, `/women-empowerment/`, `/tai-chi/`, `/street-combatives/`). The most well-resourced threat. Beats Maddog on self-defence and kids terms.
- **101 Boxing Gym** (101boxing.co.za) — thin content, single-owner coach (Dean Krossynski) site, no H2s, tiny 4-file WordPress sitemap. Structurally weak, ranks via being a real dedicated boxing gym + reviews.

Shares Maddog's Balvista Centre address (worth investigating). Edges Maddog on generic (non-kids) BJJ terms despite having zero owned-site SEO content.
- **F45 Training Ballito** (f45training.com/za/ballito) — global franchise template site, standard "Class Schedule / Success Stories / Our Fitness Team" boilerplate. Wins on franchise domain authority, not location-specific content.
- **Fit24 Gyms Ballito** (fit24gyms.co.za/ballito) — franchise site, amenity/equipment-focused ("Cardio," "Free Weights," "Indoor Running Track"), not keyword-targeted content.

## Strength / personal training
- **Grit Factory** (gritfactory.co.za) — #1 on nearly every generic PT term. Single strong page, no sitemap (one-pager), pure benefit-led copy ("Train with purpose. Build real strength."). Wins via concentrated messaging + reviews, not content depth — beatable.
- **Launch Lifestyle** (launchfit.app) — mobile-first PT brand ("Building Better Humans"), segments on one page: Private PT, Small-Group, Outdoor Classes, Kids & Youth, Kids Mountain Biking, Kids Sports Academy, **Neurodiverse Specialist Coaching** (ADHD/autism/sensory — real niche gap, Maddog doesn't address this at all), Strength & Longevity Coaching.
- **FITPOD** (fitpod.co.za) — not a single brand, a multi-instructor studio collective/marketplace, 22 pages each an individual instructor's profile (Gina G for PT, plus Pilates/dance/Zumba/kettlebell). Explains why "personal trainer ballito" surfaces Gina G specifically.
- **Body20** (body20.co.za/studio-ballito) — EMS (electrical muscle stimulation) technology differentiator, 20-min sessions, "Two-Time Studio of the Year" trust signal. Different value prop entirely, not directly competable.
- **Dalisu Goge Elite Personal Trainer** — strong in the Local Map Pack for semi-private/powerlifting-coach terms. Not yet deep-audited (no site structure pulled).

## Wellness / recovery / IV therapy
- **Kico Life** (kicolife.com) — Wix site, 30+ individually optimized service pages, one page per specific service *variant* (three ice bath temps, three NAD dose tiers, private vs. shared sauna). Wins via content depth/specificity — a directly copyable pattern for Maddog's wellness site.
- **IVology** (ivology.co.za) — multi-location (Ballito, Milnerton, Cape Town), segments content by *audience* not service ("Who We Serve: Athletes / Business Professionals / The Aging / Party Animals").
- **The IV Bar** (theivbar.co.za) — national multi-location brand, professionally Yoast-optimized, leads with "near you" phrasing directly in H1.
