Elite / Professional Track

Serious Boxing.
Serious Results.

The Olympic Boxing Programme is Maddog's elite, competitive track — full technical, tactical and conditioning preparation for athletes serious about competing. This is not a fitness class; it's a structured pathway built to develop fighters through to elite amateur and professional competition.

Coach Marcele's methodology combines traditional boxing fundamentals with modern conditioning, tactical preparation and fight intelligence — training fighters to understand why they're doing something in the ring, so they can adapt under pressure rather than relying on rehearsed combinations.
  • Full technical development — ring craft, movement, distance management
  • Tactical decision-making and defensive responsibility
  • Fight preparation — conditioning, strategy, meet-day readiness
  • A genuine pathway to national and Olympic-level competition

Private Training & Pad Work

Private Boxing Coaching.
Built Around You.

For competitive boxers, one-on-one coaching and pad work are essential tools for technical and tactical development. Every private session is designed around the individual boxer's style, strengths and areas that need work — not a generic template.

  • Improving punch accuracy, speed and combinations
  • Developing defensive reactions and counter-attacking
  • Improving footwork, distance and ring movement
  • Developing timing and rhythm
  • Building fight-specific conditioning
  • Practising tactical combinations and fight strategies
  • Correcting technical weaknesses
  • Preparing for specific opponents and competition

One-on-one training lets Coach Marcele closely monitor technique, fatigue and intensity throughout the session — suitable for complete beginners, fitness clients and competitive athletes alike, with every exercise modified to individual experience, ability and goals.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Who is the Olympic Boxing Programme for?
This is our elite, competitive track — for athletes who have a boxing foundation already (through Boxing Fitness, Kids Boxing, or prior experience) and want to move into serious competition preparation.
What age can I join the Olympic Boxing Programme?
There's no fixed age cut-off. Coach Marcele assesses every athlete individually on their boxing foundation and readiness for competitive training, so this pathway has produced everything from elite youth talent through to adult competitors.
Do I need to trial or be assessed to join?
Yes — because this is an elite training group, Coach Marcele assesses each athlete first to make sure the programme and squad are the right fit.
What does the programme include?
Full technical, tactical and conditioning preparation — ring craft, movement, distance management, defensive responsibility and tactical decision-making, built around fight preparation rather than general fitness.
What does a private or pad work session include?
Private and pad work sessions are built around you — punch accuracy, speed and combinations, defensive reactions, footwork, timing, fight-specific conditioning, tactical strategy and correcting technical weaknesses. Coach Marcele designs each session around your style, strengths and what needs work.
Has this programme produced real results?
Yes. Coach Marcele has developed national-level champions and elite youth athletes, including an undefeated youth heavyweight who has defeated national champions and is being prepared for the Olympic pathway.
How much does the Olympic Boxing Programme cost?
Group classes are R890 per month. 1-on-1 Private Training is R350 per hour, Semi Private (3 people) is R600 per hour, and Private Padwork is R700 per hour. Contact us to discuss which format is right for your level.
